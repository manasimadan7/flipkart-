Weeks 1–3 · Speaking to Shop

Can we let people shop just by talking?

Week 1Voice and audio search are game-changers for the Indian user baseDramatically expanding digital accessibility: instead of forcing users through repetitive menus and filters, we built an intuitive voice-first ordering experience.

Week 2Imagine saying, ‘Hey, I’m having a house party tonight...’The app intelligently takes care of the rest — curating party essentials, organizing categories, and streamlining checkout like magic.

Week 3My primary focus was tackling category management while balancing user agency with automation.

Weeks 4–6 · Design systems

Making the design system AI friendly

Week 4AI models usually break while following a design system — to max efficiencyDesign systems are the backbone of any digital product, supporting ’s multi-venture ecosystem. Introducing Generative AI into a strict system created friction: AI outputs don’t natively understand design tokens, causing component breaks.

Week 5What if a repository exists that makes the design workflow more designer friendly?Working with my mentors, we built a custom repository using Git and Claude that maps brand tokens straight into production code, so components plug straight into a screen.

Week 6The repository holds tokens, components and screens, making sure AI workflows and ’s design system speak the exact same language. The same screens even power small experiments like BBD (Big Billion Days) Games.

Weeks 7–8 · Enterprise

From consumer AI to enterprise scale

Week 7Building dashboards for a new enterprise ventureFor my final two weeks, I shifted gears into a brand-new enterprise venture, exploring complex operational logic, business-facing workflows, and platform architecture.

Week 8I built the dashboard: orders, fulfilment, revenue and people brought into one place, so a business team can read how the venture is doing at a glance.

Navigating Scale & High-Velocity Shipping

’s core mantra is ‘Together, We Dare to Maximise’ — taking calculated risks and solving complex e-commerce challenges at scale. Working in a fast-paced environment with rapid shipping gave me hands-on exposure across three distinct domains: Consumer AI, Design Systems, and Enterprise Platform.

Learnings

Looking back, these 8 weeks gave me a rare, comprehensive view of the end-to-end product landscape — from consumer-facing AI and deep systems engineering to enterprise infrastructure.

  1. 01

    Design for how people actually talk

    Voice turns shopping from menus and filters into a conversation. Accessibility isn’t a side feature, it’s the interface.

  2. 02

    Automation needs a steering wheel

    AI can curate a cart in seconds, but people still decide what stays. The craft is balancing agency with automation.

  3. 03

    A design system is a language

    AI only ships clean UI when it speaks the same tokens as the system. Mapping brand tokens into code made both sides fluent.

  4. 04

    Speed is a craft too

    Together, we dare to maximise: calculated risks, rapid shipping, and range across consumer AI, systems and enterprise.
